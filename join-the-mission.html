Join the mission

More ways to be in the room.

A ticket is one way in. A scholarship and a volunteer shift are two more: one for people who'd otherwise be priced out, one for people who want to help build the conference from the inside.

Volunteering

Run the conference from the inside.

Volunteers are the reason the two days actually work. In exchange for a few shifts across the event, you get a free ticket and a behind-the-scenes view of how a community conference comes together, plus a fast route into the crew who build it. It's one of the best ways to meet people if you're arriving on your own.

On the roster

What a shift looks like.

Shifts are scheduled so you still catch plenty of talks. No experience needed: we'll brief you before doors open.

🪪

Registration & welcome

Greet attendees, hand out badges, and be the friendly first face people meet when they walk in.

🎤

Session hosting

Keep a room on schedule, introduce speakers, and run the microphone during audience questions.

🧭

Info & logistics

Point people to the right room, help with the venue, and keep the day moving smoothly behind the scenes.

In return

What you get for pitching in.

🎟

A free ticket

Volunteers attend the full conference at no cost. You're part of the event, not just working it.

👋

A way in

The quickest way to meet the organizing team and other attendees, especially if you're coming solo.

🛸

Crew status

A behind-the-scenes look at how Compute! runs, and a standing invitation to help build the next one.

Diversity scholarships

Cost shouldn't decide who's in the room.

We set aside a block of scholarship places for people from groups underrepresented in open source and scientific computing, and for anyone for whom the ticket price is a real barrier: students, early-career researchers, and community contributors included. Scholarships cover a full conference ticket, and a limited number also help with travel and accommodation for those coming from further afield.

What's covered

A place, and the means to reach it.

We'd rather remove the obstacle than admire it. Here's what a scholarship can include.

🎟

Conference ticket

Full access to both days of talks, sessions, and the hallway track: the same ticket everyone else holds, at no cost to you.

🚆

Travel support

A limited number of grants toward transport for those travelling from outside the Paris region, awarded on the basis of need and distance.

🛏

Accommodation

Help with a night or two of lodging for scholarship recipients whose journey makes a same-day return impractical.

Who should apply

If you're wondering whether it's for you, apply.

There's no single profile. If any of these sound like you, we'd like to read your application.

🎓

Students & early career

People still studying, or in the first years of a career in data, research, or open source, for whom a full-price ticket is out of reach.

🌍

Underrepresented groups

Anyone from a background underrepresented at conferences like this one, who would make the room more genuinely reflective of the community.

🤝

Community contributors

Maintainers, organizers, and volunteers who give to the ecosystem but don't have an employer to expense the trip.
