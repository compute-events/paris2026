The event

Open source compute, in Paris.

Compute! brings researchers, maintainers, educators, and practitioners together to share knowledge and advance the state of the art in open data infrastructure.

🛸

Conference tracks

  • Open-source AI / ML
  • Data visualization & storytelling
  • High-performance & distributed computing
  • Interactive computing & reproducibility
  • Geospatial & instrumentation
🏛

Venue — Centre International de Conférences de Sorbonne Université, Paris

  • Main theatre: ~500 seats
  • Two side stages: ~100 seats each
  • 10 sponsor expo booths
  • 2 days on-site, November 25–26 2026
  • 3 keynotes · 42 talks · 2 lightning sessions
