About

What is Compute! Paris?

Compute! is a community and conference for everyone who works with open source compute and data infrastructure. We bring researchers, maintainers, educators and practitioners together to share knowledge, celebrate open tooling, and advance the state of the art.

Audience: students, researchers, OSS maintainers, industry engineers, and public sector practitioners.
