Ground control

A riverside venue on the Marne.

The Hôtel du Port sits right on the Marne in Nogent-sur-Marne, next to a little yacht harbor and the Bois de Vincennes, in Paris East.

Talks take place in a plush-seat amphitheater, and three bright rooms with a view of the water host the tracks and birds-of-a-feather sessions. Sponsor booths are in the panorama room overlooking the river and the harbor, where lunch, breaks and general hangout also happen.

The venue is compact, so for two days speakers, maintainers, users and sponsors are all under one roof. Every coffee break is a chance to find the person you came to meet.

The city

Paris in November.

November is a great time to be in Paris. The summer crowds are gone, the cultural calendar is full, and the city is easier to move around. Temperatures are mild, and rain adds a sparkly touch to cobblestones and cozy cafés.

Nogent-sur-Marne sits in Paris East, by the Bois de Vincennes, and has long been loved by Parisians for its guinguettes and walks along the Marne. The RER takes you straight to Châtelet in 13 minutes, so the rest of the city is never far away.
